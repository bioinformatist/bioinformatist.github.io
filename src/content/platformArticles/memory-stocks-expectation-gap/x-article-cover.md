# X Article cover

## Prompt and design brief

Create a 1600x640 editorial cover for a Chinese investment-education article
titled "我为什么不建议你买存储". Use the site's black and orange palette with
off-white supporting text. Make the two Chinese characters "存储" the dominant
visual signal. Pair them with a simplified DRAM module and an overshooting
market-price curve. Do not use topic tags, an author byline, stock photos,
logos, ticker symbols, decorative gradients, or promises of investment
returns.

## Reproduction

The checked-in implementation is deterministic SVG generated by
`build-article-assets.mjs`. Rasterize it with ImageMagick for platform use.
