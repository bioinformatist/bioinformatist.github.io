# Trading-cost distribution diagram

## Purpose

Give non-technical readers a geometric interpretation of the active-trading
expectation formula. Higher volatility widens the gross excess-return
distribution without creating forecasting edge; transaction costs shift the
net-return distribution to the loss side.

## Important limitation

The curves are conceptual normal-density illustrations. They are not fitted to
historical returns, and the argument does not assume that real stock returns
are normally distributed.

## Reproduction

Run `node build-article-assets.mjs`, then rasterize the generated SVG with
ImageMagick.
